# 📈 Wall Street AI - Investment Research Agent

> 🚀 **Learn. Research. Invest. Profit. Have Fun.**

An AI-powered investment research agent focused on **Wall Street, NASDAQ, Big Tech, semiconductors, and AI infrastructure**.

Built for education, hands-on learning, smarter investment decisions, discovering opportunities, making money, and having fun along the way! 💰

## 🎯 Mission

Build a personal AI-powered Wall Street analyst that combines financial research, market intelligence, and investment strategies to support better trading and investing decisions.

## 🧠 Research Focus

- 🏦 **Wall Street & NASDAQ** - Market trends, sentiment, and macroeconomics
- 🤖 **Big Tech** - MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, META, AAPL
- 💻 **Semiconductors** - NVDA, AMD, INTC, AVGO, QCOM, TSM
- 🚀 **AI Revolution** - GPUs, hyperscalers, data centers, AI infrastructure
- 📊 **Technical Analysis** - RSI, MACD, moving averages, volume, price action
- 💵 **Fundamental Analysis** - Earnings, valuations, growth, profitability
- 🎯 **Investment Strategies** - Swing trading and long-term investing

## ⚡ Agent Capabilities

- 🔎 Research stocks and identify investment opportunities
- 📈 Analyze technical indicators and price trends
- 📑 Evaluate earnings, financial statements, and valuations
- 🌎 Track macroeconomic developments and market catalysts
- 🐂 Build bull, base, and bear investment scenarios
- ⚖️ Assess risk/reward, entry points, and potential returns
- 🧠 Challenge assumptions with evidence-based research

## 🛠️ Project Goals

- Learn AI agents, automation, and financial data integration
- Experiment with investment research and trading strategies
- Develop practical skills in financial and technical analysis
- Build a reusable, extensible AI research assistant
- Have fun exploring markets and finding profitable opportunities! 🚀

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

This project is intended for **education, experimentation, and personal investment research**. AI-generated analysis is not guaranteed to be accurate or profitable and does not constitute professional financial advice.
